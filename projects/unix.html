Campus Ecosystem

A unified campus platform connecting students, faculty, and campus services in one digital ecosystem.

Live Demo GitHub

The Problem

Campus services are often scattered across different platforms, making it hard for students and faculty to access and collaborate efficiently.

The Solution

A single, integrated platform that brings essential campus services together with a clean, user-friendly experience.

Key Features

  • Student & faculty accounts
  • Skill sharing and help requests
  • Campus marketplace
  • Points and notifications
