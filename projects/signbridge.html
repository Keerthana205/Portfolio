SignBridge

AI Interview Platform

An AI-powered interview platform enabling real-time sign language translation and speech-to-caption conversion for accessible communication.

GitHub

The Problem

Deaf and hard-of-hearing candidates face substantial communication hurdles in standard spoken technical interviews where interpreters may not be readily available.

The Solution

SignBridge bridges the gap by translating sign gestures and captions in real time, fostering fair and accessible technical evaluation environments.

Key Features

  • Gesture recognition for interview signs
  • Bi-directional text and speech transcription
  • Dedicated real-time candidate interface
  • Accessible high-contrast WCAG UI
